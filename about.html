About NLPG

MORE THAN JUST A REPAIR SHOP

Built by a gamer, for gamers — with passion, dedication and a genuine love for the game.

Built From a Love of Gaming

Next Level Pro Gaming is not just another repair shop. NLPG was built from a genuine love for gaming, gaming hardware and the community around it.

Most of my repairs are done after hours and over weekends, when many traditional repair shops are closed. That gives me the opportunity to take the time to do things properly — with attention to detail, quality workmanship and the same care I would want for my own gaming equipment.

From console repairs and deep cleaning to controller repairs, TMR stick upgrades and hardware work, the goal is simple: provide quality service that gets your equipment back where it belongs — in the game.

REPAIR • UPGRADE • PLAY

Quality gaming repairs with a passion for the game.

The Bigger Picture

Building a Gaming Community

I don't want Next Level Pro Gaming to be just another place you take your console when something goes wrong. The bigger goal is to build something that brings gamers and like-minded people together.

A community where we can share knowledge, help each other out, talk gaming, show off our setups, discover new ideas and simply enjoy being part of the gaming world.

And this is only the beginning.

What's Coming

More Is On The Way

The Next Level Pro Gaming app is already in the works, with the aim of making repairs, updates and customer communication even easier.

A dedicated Discord community is also planned, along with more projects, services and ideas that will continue growing NLPG beyond the repair bench.

There is a lot more to come — and this is just the start of Next Level Pro Gaming.
