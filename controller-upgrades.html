TMR STICK UPGRADES

A dedicated page for one of NLPG's most popular controller upgrades.

Why TMR?

TMR joystick technology uses magnetic sensing rather than the traditional resistive track used by many potentiometer sticks. The goal is a more durable stick-sensing solution and reduced exposure to traditional potentiometer wear.

  • Reduced traditional stick-drift wear
  • Smoother stick sensing
  • Professional installation
  • Controller tested after the upgrade

Compatible Controllers

Compatibility depends on the exact controller model and revision. Send NLPG a photo or model number and we'll confirm whether the upgrade is suitable.
